Trip philosophy

  • Architecture and beautiful neighborhoods over museums
  • Walking and wandering rather than rushing between attractions
  • Great food, wine, cocktails, and local beer
  • A relaxed pace with cafés and people-watching
  • Hidden local gems mixed with a few iconic sights
  • No more than one organized activity per day
  • Strategic use of trams, funiculars, Bolt, Uber, and taxis instead of unnecessary hill climbing
  • Apartment resets before important dinners
  • Room for spontaneous discoveries
  • Skip rather than rush when the day becomes too full
Bordeaux host schedule is tentative

Bordeaux activities are supplied by the host and remain subject to change unless they are explicitly marked confirmed.

Laundry plan

Pack for repeat outfits. Plan laundry at the end of Bordeaux or the beginning of Lisbon.

Food balance

Do not overdo bacalhau. Try one good cod preparation, then diversify across tinned fish, seafood, pork, rissóis, bifanas/francesinha, vegetables, and other regional dishes.
