Four useful distinctions

The model is only part of the tool.

These terms are related, but they answer different questions. Keeping them separate makes the comparison more honest.

01

Personalization

How the product adapts to you: your preferences, history, work context, or remembered conventions.

02

Customization

How you shape reusable behavior through instructions, knowledge, skills, tools, agents, or workflows.

03

Scaffolding

The starter structure used to assemble a solution: templates, files, conventions, and generated building blocks.

04

Harness

The operating environment around the model that supplies context, tools, memory, permissions, triggers, and guardrails.
